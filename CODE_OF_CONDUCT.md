# Code of conduct

Project spaces must remain professional, inclusive, and focused on constructive
engineering collaboration.

- Treat participants with respect regardless of background, identity,
  experience, or viewpoint.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Do not publish another person's private data, credentials, resume, project
  content, or security report.
- Do not use project channels for harassment, threats, spam, deceptive promotion,
  or discriminatory conduct.
- Respect intellectual-property, competition, client, and confidentiality
  obligations.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
needed to protect contributors and the project. Report conduct concerns through
the least public repository channel available; do not disclose sensitive details
in an issue.
